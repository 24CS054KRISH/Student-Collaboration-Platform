# Literature Survey

## Existing Methods

Currently, students mostly use WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, and personal contacts to find teammates and project members. These platforms are useful for communication but do not provide a structured way to build project teams.

## Limitations

- No dedicated platform for student team formation.
- Difficult to find students based on skills.
- No proper project discovery system.
- Team management is not organized.
- No AI-based recommendation system.

## Proposed Improvement

Student Collaboration Platform provides a centralized solution where students can create profiles, discover projects, find teammates based on skills and interests, communicate with team members, and receive AI-based recommendations.
